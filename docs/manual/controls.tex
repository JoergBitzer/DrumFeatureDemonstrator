Control & Range & Default & Description \\
\hline
Base note & 24 - 72 & 60 & MIDI note at which the sample plays at its original speed (60 = C4); range C1 - C5. \\
Start & 0.0000 - 1.0000 & 0.0000 & Start of the played region, as a fraction of the sample length. \\
End & 0.0000 - 1.0000 & 1.0000 & End of the played region, as a fraction of the sample length. \\
Pattern length & 8 steps - 32 steps & 16 steps & Number of 1/16 steps of the pattern (8 - 32). \\
Step note value & 1/1 - 1/64 triplet & 1/16 & The note value that one step of the pattern represents: 1/1 to 1/64, each normal, dotted or as a triplet. \\
Sync to host & 0 - 1 & 1 & 1 = follow the tempo and position of the DAW, 0 = internal tempo and play button. \\
Internal tempo & 40.0 bpm - 240.0 bpm & 120.0 bpm & Tempo of the internal beat (used if Sync to host is off). \\
Internal play & 0 - 1 & 0 & Plays (1) or pauses (0) the internal beat (used if Sync to host is off). \\
Fade in & 0.000 s - 10.000 s & 0.000 s & Fade-in time at the start of the played region (the top handle at the start line of the waveform). \\
Fade out & 0.000 s - 10.000 s & 0.000 s & Fade-out time at the end of the played region (the top handle at the end line of the waveform). \\
Declick & 0 - 1 & 0 & If on, the start and end handles snap to a zero crossing of the sample when you move them (no click at the region edges). \\
